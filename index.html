Wiener Dog in Space

You play as Laika the dachshund.

The astronaut is unconscious inside the ship and can't move.

Move: WASD or Arrow Keys   Run: hold Shift

Explore: walk off the left or right edge outside

Enter / exit ship: walk up (W) into the door

Interact (monitor, pick up, fill oxygen, attach): E

Bark: B

Hide this panel: H   Hide objectives: O
